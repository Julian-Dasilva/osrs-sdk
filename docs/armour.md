[Divine rune pouch revision 15359154](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Divine_rune_pouch?oldid=15359154) weighs 0.878 kg. It is an inventory item without rune storage or casting simulation.

Elite void requires the matching helmet, elite top, elite robe and void knight gloves. The [melee helmet revision 15260466](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Void_melee_helm?oldid=15260466) gives 1.1 effective Attack and Strength. The [ranger helmet revision 15260468](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Void_ranger_helm?oldid=15260468) gives 1.1 effective ranged accuracy and 1.125 effective ranged strength with the elite set. Effective levels round down before the equipment bonus. Incomplete sets and the other attack style receive no bonus.

Book of the Dead occupies the offhand slot. Thralls are not simulated. Magma helm poison and venom, Eclipse Moon burn and degradation, Ghommal's hilt teleports and task perks, and rune pouch storage are not used by the Ocellus loadouts and are not simulated. The Eclipse set would also require the helm and atlatl, which these loadouts do not carry.
